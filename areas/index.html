Areas we cover in the West Midlands

We match specialists on the property postcode, not the town name, because postcodes cross council boundaries. Each page below covers local cases and the council licensing rules that affect re-letting.

  • BirminghamB1 to B48 (Birmingham city council area; some B postcodes belong to Sandwell, Solihull or Walsall)
  • WolverhamptonWV1 to WV11 and WV14 (Bilston); WV12 and WV13 (Willenhall) are in Walsall
  • CoventryCV1 to CV6, plus parts of CV7 and CV8
  • WalsallWS1 to WS10 (WS10 Wednesbury is in Sandwell)
  • DudleyDY1 to DY3, DY5, DY6, DY8, DY9, plus B62 and B63 (Halesowen)
  • SandwellB64 to B71 (Cradley Heath, Rowley Regis, Smethwick, Oldbury, West Bromwich), DY4 (Tipton) and WS10 (Wednesbury)

Elsewhere in the West Midlands, such as Solihull, use the form. If we have a vetted specialist covering your postcode, we will tell you. If we do not, we will say so.
