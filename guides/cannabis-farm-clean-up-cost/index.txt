<h2>What pushes the price up</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Number of rooms used</strong> and whether the loft was used</li>
<li><strong>Electrical tampering</strong>: a bypassed meter almost always means a full test, and often a rewire</li>
<li><strong>How long the grow ran</strong>: longer means more damp, mould and plaster damage</li>
<li><strong>Structural alterations</strong>: holes in ceilings, walls and floors, removed doors</li>
<li><strong>Access</strong>: flats above shops, city-centre parking and skip permits</li>
<li><strong>Waste volume and type</strong>: electrical items and chemicals cost more to dispose of properly</li>
</ul>
