22:Tb75,<h2>Typical damage after a grow</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Electrics</strong>: bypassed meters, extra circuits, overloaded sockets and cable run through ceilings. Usually needs a full test and often a partial or full rewire.</li>
<li><strong>Ceilings and walls</strong>: holes for ducting and cables, screw and staple damage, plaster softened by months of humidity.</li>
<li><strong>Damp and mould</strong>: grows run hot and wet. Mould in plaster, behind skirting and under flooring is common.</li>
<li><strong>Floors</strong>: water and soil damage to carpets and boards. In the mydeposits case we cite, floorboards needed repair.</li>
<li><strong>Windows and doors</strong>: sealed or boarded windows, removed internal doors, damaged frames. The same case included replacing windows and reinstalling lounge doors.</li>
<li><strong>Locks</strong>: keys are rarely returned. Change them as soon as you have lawful possession.</li>
</ul>
<h2>How a coordinated restoration runs</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Survey and scope.</strong> A written scope of works, room by room, with photos. This is what your insurer or loss adjuster will price against.</li>
<li><strong>Clean-up and strip-out.</strong> See <a href="/mercia-property-recovery/services/cannabis-farm-clean-up/">cannabis farm clean-up</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Electrical make-safe, testing and repairs</strong> by a registered electrician, finishing with a new Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR).</li>
<li><strong>Damp and mould remediation</strong>, then drying out before any plastering.</li>
<li><strong>Building repairs</strong>: plastering, ceilings, flooring, windows, doors and joinery.</li>
<li><strong>Decoration and final clean.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Handover pack</strong>: invoices, certificates, waste transfer notes and before-and-after photos.</li>
</ol>
<h2>Why one coordinator matters</h2>
<p>When three or four trades work independently, the gaps show up as delays: the plasterer arrives before the walls have dried, or the electrician is waiting for ceilings to be open. A single specialist who manages the sequence usually saves weeks of void time, and gives you one invoice trail for the insurer.</p>
<h2>Licensing and re-letting</h2>
<p>If your property is in one of the 25 Birmingham wards under selective licensing, or is a licensed HMO anywhere in the region, the council expects it to meet licence conditions before it is re-let. A current EICR is required for private rented homes in England. Keep the certificates from the restoration: you will need them.</p>
<h2>Rough cost</h2>
<p>One published breakdown puts the combined cost of rewiring, structural repairs, plumbing and heating, damp and mould, cleaning and admin at <strong>£10,800 to £26,500 or more</strong>. That is one firm&#39;s estimate, not a quote. Our <a href="/mercia-property-recovery/guides/cannabis-farm-clean-up-cost/">cost guide</a> lists each line and its source.</p>
