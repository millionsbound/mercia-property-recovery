22:T8ad,<h2>Residential and commercial are different</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Residential buildings.</strong> Squatting in a residential building is a criminal offence in England and Wales under section 144 of the Legal Aid, Sentencing and Punishment of Offenders Act 2012. Report it to the police on 101, or 999 if there is a risk to anyone.</li>
<li><strong>Commercial buildings and land.</strong> Squatting in a commercial property is usually a civil matter. The owner normally needs a court order. Where you act quickly, an <strong>Interim Possession Order</strong> can be applied for, but the application must be made within 28 days of finding out about the occupation.</li>
<li><strong>Former tenants are not squatters.</strong> If the person was given a tenancy or licence, even one that has ended, you must follow the possession process. Changing the locks or removing them yourself can be unlawful eviction.</li>
</ul>
<h2>What we can arrange</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>An introduction to an enforcement firm</strong> for commercial premises and land, or to a solicitor where a court application is needed. Solicitors are regulated by the SRA. If they pay or receive a referral fee, they must tell you.</li>
<li><strong>Clearance</strong> of rubbish, furniture and drug paraphernalia by a registered waste carrier.</li>
<li><strong>A biohazard or needle sweep</strong> if needed. See <a href="/mercia-property-recovery/services/drug-den-needle-clean-up/">drug den and needle clean-up</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Securing the building</strong>: lock changes, boarding or steel screens, and alarms, so it does not happen again.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Rough cost</h2>
<p>For land, one enforcement firm publishes <strong>£650 plus VAT</strong> to serve a notice to vacate and <strong>£330 plus VAT</strong> for the first hour per agent for an eviction. One solicitor publishes a fixed fee of about <strong>£2,600 plus VAT</strong> for a commercial possession claim through to vacant possession. Clearance and security are priced separately.</p>
<h2>What we will not do</h2>
<p>We will not help remove anyone who may be a tenant without the proper process, and we will not get involved in any approach that relies on force or intimidation.</p>
