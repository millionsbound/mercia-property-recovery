<h2>After every introduction</h2>
<p>We follow up with you a week and a month after an introduction to ask whether the work went ahead and how it went. If a firm does not respond quickly, does poor work, or treats a customer badly, we stop introducing them.</p>
<h2>What we cannot promise</h2>
<p>Checks reduce risk; they do not remove it. You contract with the specialist directly, so read their terms, get a written quote and scope, and do not pay large sums up front. If something goes wrong, tell us. See our <a href="/mercia-property-recovery/complaints/">complaints process</a>.</p>
