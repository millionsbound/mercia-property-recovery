<h2>Sandwell&#39;s licensing rules</h2>
<p>Sandwell has a <strong>borough-wide additional HMO licensing scheme</strong> for properties let to three or four people from two or more households who share facilities. It runs until 30 September 2029. Mandatory licensing covers larger HMOs. Sandwell does not currently operate selective licensing for single-household lets. If your property is an HMO, it will need to meet licence conditions after the restoration.</p>
<h2>The six towns</h2>
<p>We cover the whole borough: Oldbury, West Bromwich, Smethwick, Tipton, Wednesbury and Rowley Regis, including Cradley Heath. Postcodes cross council boundaries here (for example, some B postcodes are Birmingham and some are Sandwell), so we match specialists on the actual postcode, not the town name.</p>
