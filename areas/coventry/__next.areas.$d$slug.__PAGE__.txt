<ul>
<li><strong>Found by a letting agent, July 2026.</strong> The Negotiator reported that a Coventry letting agent forced entry to a property over rent arrears and found around 380 plants and a bypassed electricity meter. <h2>Coventry&#39;s licensing rules</h2>
<p>Coventry&#39;s <strong>city-wide additional HMO licensing scheme</strong> started on 4 May 2025 and runs until 3 May 2030. It covers HMOs with three or four occupants and most section 257 HMOs (certain converted blocks of flats), alongside mandatory licensing for larger HMOs. If your property is an HMO, the restoration must leave it compliant with licence conditions before you re-let.</p>
<h2>What the Coventry cases show</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Arrears and no access</strong> are often the first sign. The Canley and arrears cases both came to light that way.</li>
<li><strong>Forced entry must be lawful.</strong> If a tenancy is still running, take advice before entering without consent. Where you believe a crime is in progress, call the police.</li>
<li><strong>Student and HMO stock</strong> around the city centre and the universities turns over quickly. Plan restorations around the academic year if you can.</li>
</ul>
