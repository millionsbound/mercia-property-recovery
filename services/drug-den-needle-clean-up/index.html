Drug den and needle clean-up

When a property has been used for drug taking, the danger is often hidden: needles in sofas, under carpets, in loft insulation and behind bath panels. This is not a job for a general cleaner. We introduce you to biohazard specialists who do needle sweeps and deep cleans with the right equipment and disposal.

Get a recovery quoteor call 07498 603180

When to call a specialist

  • You have found, or suspect, used needles anywhere in the property
  • There are bodily fluids, blood or heavy soiling
  • The property has been occupied by squatters or used by people other than the tenant
  • You are a letting agent and need to make a void property safe for staff, inventory clerks and trades

What the job usually involves

  1. A sharps sweep of every room, including soft furnishings, under flooring and in the loft, using puncture-resistant gloves and litter pickers.
  2. Sharps placed in approved containers and taken away for incineration as clinical waste, with paperwork.
  3. Biohazard cleaning of contaminated surfaces with appropriate disinfectants.
  4. Removal of contaminated items such as mattresses and carpets.
  5. A written record of what was found and removed, which is useful for your insurer, your deposit claim and your own records.

Keep your staff safe

If you or your staff find a needle, do not try to recap it, do not put it in a normal bin, and do not reach into places you cannot see. Leave it, close the room if you can, and call a specialist. If someone is injured by a needle, they should wash the wound under running water, let it bleed, and get urgent medical advice the same day.

Rough cost

One West Midlands specialist publishes needle sweeps from £250. Larger clean-ups depend heavily on the size of the property and what is found, so expect a site visit before a firm price.

Questions landlords ask

Do I need to tell the police?

If you think a crime has been committed or the property is still being used, call 101, or 999 in an emergency. The specialist's job is to clean, not to investigate.

Can the specialist clear the rest of the property at the same time?

Many can. Say so on the form and we will look for a firm that does both biohazard work and general clearance.
