For letting agents and property managers

When a managed property turns out to be a cannabis farm, your landlord calls you first. You need to give them a clear next step that day, not a list of numbers to ring. That is what we are for.

Get a recovery quoteor call 07498 603180

How it works for agents

  1. You or your landlord call us or use the form. Give us the postcode, what was found and whether the police have released the property.
  2. We call back and qualify the job so the specialist knows what they are walking into: electrics, waste, damp, access, insurer involvement.
  3. We introduce up to two vetted specialists who cover the postcode. Your landlord chooses, agrees the price and pays them directly.
  4. You get a copy of the paperwork if your landlord agrees: photos, scope of works, waste transfer notes, EICR and invoices. That makes the insurance and deposit side easier for everyone.

What we check before we introduce anyone

See how we vet specialists. In short: company details, waste carrier registration, insurance certificates, electrical registration where relevant, and references.

Prevention: the inspection condition

A published Financial Ombudsman decision (DRN-6210910) upheld an insurer's refusal of a cannabis farm claim because inspections had not been carried out every three months, as the policy required. If your management agreement says six-monthly inspections but your landlords' policies say three-monthly, that gap is a risk for them and a conversation for you. Our inspection trap guide sets out the decision in plain English. You are welcome to link to it or share it with landlords.

Referral arrangements

We are happy to discuss a referral arrangement with agents. If one is agreed, it will be in writing, and you will need to tell your landlords about it, as we do on our About page. We do not pay for introductions that are not disclosed.

Get in touch

Use the request form. We will ask on the call whether you are the agent or the landlord, and who should hear from the specialist.

Questions landlords ask

Do you replace our contractors?

No. If you already have a contractor you trust for this kind of work, use them. We are useful when you do not, or when the job needs a specialist you do not have on your list.

Will you contact our landlord directly?

Only with their consent, given on the form or to you. We do not market to your landlords.
