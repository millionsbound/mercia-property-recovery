Complaints

If something has gone wrong with our service, or with a specialist we introduced you to, we want to know.

Get a recovery quoteor call 07498 603180

How to complain

Use the request form. Tell us what happened, the property postcode and, if it is about a specialist, which firm.

What we will do

  • Acknowledge your complaint within 2 working days.
  • Look into it, including asking the specialist for their side if the complaint is about them.
  • Give you a written response within 14 days, or tell you why it will take longer.

Complaints about a specialist's work

Your contract is with the specialist, so they are responsible for putting things right. We will raise it with them and, if we are not satisfied with how they respond, we will stop introducing them. Many trade bodies, such as NICEIC and NAPIT for electrical work, have their own complaints processes.
