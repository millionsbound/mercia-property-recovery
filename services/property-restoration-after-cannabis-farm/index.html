Cannabis farm damage repair and full property restoration

Clearing a cannabis farm is the cheap part. Getting the property back to a lettable, licensable standard usually means electrical work, damp and mould treatment, building repairs and redecoration. We introduce you to specialists who can run the whole job and give you one scope of works for your insurer.

Typical damage after a grow

  • Electrics: bypassed meters, extra circuits, overloaded sockets and cable run through ceilings. Usually needs a full test and often a partial or full rewire.
  • Ceilings and walls: holes for ducting and cables, screw and staple damage, plaster softened by months of humidity.
  • Damp and mould: grows run hot and wet. Mould in plaster, behind skirting and under flooring is common.
  • Floors: water and soil damage to carpets and boards. In the mydeposits case we cite, floorboards needed repair.
  • Windows and doors: sealed or boarded windows, removed internal doors, damaged frames. The same case included replacing windows and reinstalling lounge doors.
  • Locks: keys are rarely returned. Change them as soon as you have lawful possession.

How a coordinated restoration runs

  1. Survey and scope. A written scope of works, room by room, with photos. This is what your insurer or loss adjuster will price against.
  2. Clean-up and strip-out. See cannabis farm clean-up.
  3. Electrical make-safe, testing and repairs by a registered electrician, finishing with a new Electrical Installation Condition Report (EICR).
  4. Damp and mould remediation, then drying out before any plastering.
  5. Building repairs: plastering, ceilings, flooring, windows, doors and joinery.
  6. Decoration and final clean.
  7. Handover pack: invoices, certificates, waste transfer notes and before-and-after photos.

Why one coordinator matters

When three or four trades work independently, the gaps show up as delays: the plasterer arrives before the walls have dried, or the electrician is waiting for ceilings to be open. A single specialist who manages the sequence usually saves weeks of void time, and gives you one invoice trail for the insurer.

Licensing and re-letting

If your property is in one of the 25 Birmingham wards under selective licensing, or is a licensed HMO anywhere in the region, the council expects it to meet licence conditions before it is re-let. A current EICR is required for private rented homes in England. Keep the certificates from the restoration: you will need them.

Rough cost

One published breakdown puts the combined cost of rewiring, structural repairs, plumbing and heating, damp and mould, cleaning and admin at £10,800 to £26,500 or more. That is one firm's estimate, not a quote. Our cost guide lists each line and its source.

Questions landlords ask

Will my insurer let me choose the contractor?

Some will, some will want to use their own panel or send a loss adjuster first. Check before you sign anything. A detailed scope from a specialist is useful either way.

How long does a full restoration take?

It depends on the drying time and the size of the property. The specialist will give you a programme after the survey. Ask for it in writing.

Can the work start before the insurance decision?

Making the property safe and secure usually can, and insurers generally expect you to prevent further damage. Larger repairs are best agreed with the insurer first if you intend to claim.
