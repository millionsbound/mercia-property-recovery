<h2>Small grows, real damage</h2>
<p>A one or two-room grow sounds minor, but the damage is similar in kind:</p>
<ul>
<li>the meter or wiring is often tampered with, as in the Sedgley case</li>
<li>the room has been sealed, so damp and mould build up quickly</li>
<li>sheeting, staples and screws damage walls and ceilings</li>
</ul>
<p>The clean-up is smaller, but the electrical checks and the paperwork for your insurer are the same.</p>
<h2>Neighbours and reputation</h2>
<p>Many Dudley finds follow reports from neighbours. Once the property is released, neighbours will notice the clean-up. A discreet firm, unmarked vans where possible, and a short, polite word with immediate neighbours about when work will happen usually helps.</p>
<h2>Licensing</h2>
<p>Mandatory HMO licensing applies across the borough. Check with Dudley Council&#39;s private sector housing team if you are unsure whether your property needs a licence.</p>
