How we vet the specialists we introduce

We only introduce firms that have passed the checks below. We repeat them every year, and sooner if a certificate expires or a customer tells us something has gone wrong.

Get a recovery quoteor call 07498 603180

The checks

Check How we check it
The business exists and is who it says it is Companies House record for limited companies; trading address and owner's ID for sole traders and partnerships
Waste carrier registration The Environment Agency public register. Any firm removing waste must be registered
Public liability insurance A copy of the certificate, with the expiry date recorded
Employer's liability insurance A copy of the certificate if they employ staff
Electrical work Registration with a competent person scheme such as NICEIC or NAPIT, checked on the scheme's public register
Experience with this kind of job Photos of previous jobs, with addresses and faces removed, and a description of their method
References At least two recent customers, or a track record of genuine public reviews
Staff checks We ask whether staff working in occupied or vulnerable settings are DBS checked

After every introduction

We follow up with you a week and a month after an introduction to ask whether the work went ahead and how it went. If a firm does not respond quickly, does poor work, or treats a customer badly, we stop introducing them.

What we cannot promise

Checks reduce risk; they do not remove it. You contract with the specialist directly, so read their terms, get a written quote and scope, and do not pay large sums up front. If something goes wrong, tell us. See our complaints process.
