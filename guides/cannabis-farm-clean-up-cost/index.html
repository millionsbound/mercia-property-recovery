What pushes the price up

  • Number of rooms used and whether the loft was used
  • Electrical tampering: a bypassed meter almost always means a full test, and often a rewire
  • How long the grow ran: longer means more damp, mould and plaster damage
  • Structural alterations: holes in ceilings, walls and floors, removed doors
  • Access: flats above shops, city-centre parking and skip permits
  • Waste volume and type: electrical items and chemicals cost more to dispose of properly

How to get a real price

Ask for a survey, a written scope room by room, and an itemised quote. If you are claiming, check with your insurer before you accept. Tell us about the property and we will introduce you to up to two specialists who cover your area.
